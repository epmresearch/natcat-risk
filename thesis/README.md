# Thesis materials

Suggested thesis outline (subject to university requirements and supervisor approval):
1. Introduction and research questions
2. Literature review (landslide risk, road closure, indirect losses, resilience)
3. Case study and data
4. Methods (hazard, exposure, vulnerability, impact, road network, economics)
5. Results (direct, indirect, annual risk, mitigation)
6. Discussion, uncertainty and limitations
7. Conclusion and future work

Commit text-based chapters, source references and selected figures as appropriate. Follow the department's formal thesis format when confirmed.
